The recipe editor

Write it the way you'd cook it.

A recipe on Simmer is not a caption under a photo. It has sections in the order a cook thinks — cover, dish, time, ingredients, steps — and each one is a real field, so the person reading it three streets away can actually make the thing.

  • Ingredients as quantity · unit · name, one row each
  • Steps with their own photo or short clip
  • Prep time, cook time, servings
  • A cover photo or video
  • A full cook-along video, if you want one
  • Notes, and nutrition per serving if you know it

Photos and videos come straight from your library. Simmer never asks for the camera or the microphone.

A mascot, not a name

Nobody sees who you are

Design a cook — body, colours, hat, apron, the utensil in their hand — or pick a ready-made one. Your real name appears nowhere, and your handle is the only thing anyone else ever sees on a post.

Your street stays yours

A neighbourhood, never a pin

Others see a coarse label and a rough distance — “Camden, about 4 miles”. Your exact position works out whose recipes you see and is never shown to another person, on a map or anywhere else.

Reactions and tips

Love, Yum, Fire, Wow — then SPICE

Four reactions, one per recipe, switch it any time. When a post deserves more, tip SPICE: earn it by cooking, keeping a streak, finishing quests or watching a short ad, or buy a pack. SPICE has no cash value and cannot be withdrawn.

Also in the app

Eat in a way you can keep up.

Simmer carries a healthy-eating challenge built on Abbey Sharp's Hunger Crushing Combo: protein, fibre, fat, and something you actually want. It asks one question — what could you add to make this more satisfying? — wherever you actually are, at the stove or over the sink at 9pm.

  • No calorie counting
  • No macro tracking
  • No weights, no targets, no score
  • “Log it as it is” is a real answer

For cooks

Your recipes, in front of your neighbours.

You don't need a studio, a crew or a following somewhere else. You need a pan, a phone and the recipe written down properly. Simmer puts it in front of the people closest to you, and the ones who cook it send SPICE back.

  • Post the recipe, not a reel — quantities travel further than vibes
  • Put the dish in the title, and the trick in step three
  • Every tip on a post reaches the cook who wrote it
  • Share to Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or X — the photo and a link go with it
  • Keep the streak — the daily reward grows with it
  • Quests, XP and a leaderboard of cooks nearby

SPICE is in-app only today. It has no cash value and cannot be withdrawn or transferred. Paying cooks real money is what we want to build next, and it needs a payout system that doesn't exist yet — we'd rather say so than imply otherwise.
